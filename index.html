    How is this worked out?

    The estimate is the average cost to make an item at least this good from scratch. One try = a clean item plus the same scrolls. A try works only if the clean roll is at least as good and the scrolls pass at least as many times (dark scrolls can also destroy the item).

    estimate = (clean price + scroll cost) ÷ chance a try works

    Players don't always sell at rebuild cost, so treat it as a ballpark, and check what similar items sell for.
