      How is the rarity worked out?

      Natural roll. A dropped item's stats roll around its default: ±10% of it (at most ±5, or ±10 for DEF, HP and MP), every value equally likely. For each stat your clean item rolled above the default, we take the chance a drop rolls it at least that high, and multiply them. Stats at or below the default don't make it rarer.

      roll chance (one stat) = (max − value + 1) ÷ (max − min + 1)
natural roll = product over the stats above default

      Scrolls. The chance the scrolls pass like this in the slots used: each pass has the scroll's success rate, and failed slots are spread over the scrolls in the most likely way (a failed Chaos scroll destroys the item half the time, so the item must also have survived).

      scrolls = for each scroll used:
  sum over k ≥ passed of
    C(used, k) × p^k × ((1 − p) × (1 − boom))^(used − k)

      Rarity = natural roll × scrolls, shown as "1 in N": Common (under 1 in 5), Uncommon (1 in 5+), Rare (1 in 50+), Epic (1 in 500+), Legendary (1 in 5,000+).

      Scroll rates in MapleStory Classic: Lesser 100%, Intermediate 60%, Greater 10%, Chaos 10% (half of Chaos failures destroy the item). Exact gains per scroll come from the game data.
