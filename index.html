      How is this worked out?

      The estimate is the average cost to make this item from scratch. One try = a clean item with these stats plus the same scrolls. A try works only if the scrolls pass at least as many times (dark scrolls can also destroy the item). Failed slots are worked out from the slots left.

      estimate = (clean price + scroll cost) ÷ chance a try works

      Players don't always sell at rebuild cost, so treat it as a ballpark, and check what similar items sell for.
