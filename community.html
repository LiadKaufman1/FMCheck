    How it works

    • Put an item up: work it out in the calculator, then press Publish under the estimate.
    • For sale items stay on the list for 3 days (7 for supporters). Press Still selling under My items to keep yours up.
    • ✓ Checked on screen: the seller showed the item's window from the game, and the stats and slots matched. Reading happens in the seller's browser: the screen is never uploaded. It's a strong hint, not a guarantee, so look at the item in game before you pay.
    • The estimate comes from the seller's prices when they published. Compare it with the asking price.
    • ⭐ Featured items were boosted by FMCheck supporters for 24 hours. Featured is marked clearly; it doesn't change the stats, the badges or the estimate.
    • Mesos only. Real-money trading isn't allowed. See something fake? Press Report on the item.
