      How it works

      • Put an item up: enter it under Sell an item (item, clean stats, slots left, scrolls that passed), then press Publish. You set the asking price, or leave it empty to just show the item off. Entering prices for an estimate is optional. You can have 3 items up at once (10 as a supporter).
      • The tabs: For sale is what's up for sale now. Rarest this week is everything added in the last 7 days, rarest first. My items is yours, with the buttons to manage them.
      • For sale items stay on the list for 3 days (7 for supporters). Press Still selling under My items to keep yours up. Items are listed by when they were first posted: Still selling, edits or posting the same item again don't move it up.
      • Checked on screen: the seller showed the item's window from the game, and the stats and slots matched. The chance shown for each item is worked out by FMCheck from its stats, not typed by the seller. Reading happens in the seller's browser: the screen is never uploaded. It's a strong hint, not a guarantee, so look at the item in game before you pay.
      • The estimate, when there is one, comes from prices the seller entered when publishing, so treat it as theirs. Sellers set any price they like; "Above estimate" warns when the asking price is above even their own estimate. For your own check, open the item under Sell an item and enter today's prices.
      • Featured items were boosted by FMCheck supporters for 24 hours: they're in the Featured row and first in every list and search they match. Featured is marked clearly; it doesn't change the stats, the badges or the estimate.
      • Mesos only. Real-money trading isn't allowed. See something fake? Press Report on the item.
