Accessibility statement

Last updated: 5 November 2026

We want FMCheck to be usable by everyone. The site aims to meet WCAG 2.1 level AA (the basis of Israeli Standard 5568) and is checked with automated accessibility tests.

  • Keyboard: everything works with the keyboard; a "Skip to the calculator" link comes first, and focus is always visible.
  • Screen readers: form fields have labels, the item search is announced as a list of suggestions, and the price and chance update aloud when they change. The item card image has a full text description.
  • Colors: text meets AA contrast in light and dark mode.
  • Accessibility options (the round button at the bottom left): larger text, stop animations, high contrast and underlined links. Your choice is saved in your browser.
  • Motion: the floating background stops by itself if your device is set to reduce motion.
  • Mobile: the layout works on small screens and with zoom.

Known limits: game icons and sprites are images from the game and have no text of their own; the item names next to them say what they are. The background animation is decorative only.

Found something hard to use? Tell us on the FMCheck Discord and we'll fix it.
