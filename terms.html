Privacy

  • No ads or ad trackers. Working out an item's price works without an account.
  • The prices you type are saved in your own browser (local storage), so you don't retype them; clear your browser's site data to remove them. They're also sent to us anonymously (the item, the price and the time; your IP address only as a one-way code that changes every day), to keep FMCheck's price list current: once 3 or more players typed a price for an item, their middle price is used. Typed prices are deleted after 30 days.
  • Accounts. Signing in with Google is optional and only needed to post items. We don't store your email, name or photo: only a one-way code made from your Google account id, so we can recognize you next time, and the in-game name you type. Sign-in uses a cookie that's needed to keep you signed in (no tracking). Google's sign-in button is loaded from Google when you open a sign-in, and Google's own privacy policy applies to it.
  • Items you post (the item, its stats, slots and scrolls, your price if you add one, channel, FM room, note and in-game name) are public on FMCheck until you remove them. Prices people add are also kept to understand the market better later on. Delete account removes your account and all your items at once. Rare items may also be posted to the FMCheck Discord.
  • Supporter payments go through Ko-fi (and its card or PayPal processors); we never see card details. Ko-fi tells us the payment, the message you wrote and the email you paid with. We keep only a one-way code of that email, to match monthly renewals to your account, and never the email itself.
  • Check it on screen. When you share your game window, the picture is read inside your own browser and never uploaded or saved. Only the numbers read from it (stats and slots left) are sent, to compare with your item. Sharing stops when you press Stop or close the window.
  • Visit counts. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies and without tracking you across sites.
  • Hosting. The site runs on Cloudflare, which may briefly process technical data such as your IP address to deliver and protect the site.
  • Fonts and icons are served from this site; your browser doesn't contact Google or other font services.
  • Links to other sites (meso.watch, Discord, PayPal, OSMS) follow those sites' own privacy policies once you click them. Donations are handled entirely by PayPal; we never see your payment details.

Accessibility statement

Last updated: 5 November 2026

We want FMCheck to be usable by everyone. The site aims to meet WCAG 2.1 level AA (the basis of Israeli Standard 5568) and is checked with automated accessibility tests.

  • Keyboard: everything works with the keyboard; a "Skip to selling an item" link comes first, and focus is always visible.
  • Screen readers: form fields have labels, the item search is announced as a list of suggestions, and the price and chance update aloud when they change. The item card image has a full text description.
  • Colors: text meets AA contrast in light and dark mode.
  • Accessibility options (the round button at the bottom right): larger text, stop animations, high contrast and underlined links. Your choice is saved in your browser.
  • Motion: the floating background stops by itself if your device is set to reduce motion.
  • Mobile: the layout works on small screens and with zoom.

Known limits: game icons and sprites are images from the game and have no text of their own; the item names next to them say what they are. The background animation is decorative only.

Found something hard to use? Tell us on the FMCheck Discord and we'll fix it.
